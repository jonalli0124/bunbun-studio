bunbun · first-time setup

Set up your bunbun

This wipes the toy completely and puts a fresh one on it — the firmware, a ready-made world to live in, and a brand-new egg. Nothing to download and nothing to install. It takes about two minutes.

This browser can't talk to the toy. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer — phones and tablets can't do it. This page has to be opened over https to reach the cable.

A small window will ask which device to use — see below for which one.

Already flashed? Put it on your wifi

Use this straight after the install finishes — it saves joining the toy's own network. Leave the cable in.

Which one do I pick?

The window that opens lists everything plugged into your computer. The toy is the one called:

USB JTAG/serial debug unit

On a Mac it may be USB JTAG/serial debug unit or usbmodem followed by some numbers. On Windows it will have a COM number after it, like (COM26) — the number is different on every computer and doesn't matter.

Still not sure? Close the window, unplug the toy, and open it again. Whichever entry vanished is the toy. Plug it back in and pick that one.

Picking the wrong one is harmless — it simply fails to connect and nothing is written. Close it and try the other.

What happens

  1. Plug the toy into your computer Use a USB cable that carries data — a charge-only cable will not show up. Plug it into the toy's own socket, not a separate power brick.
  2. Click the button and choose the device Look for USB JTAG/serial debug unit. Getting it wrong is harmless — it just fails to connect, and you try the other one.
  3. Say yes to erasing It offers to wipe the device first. Say yes — that is the whole point, and it is what makes this a clean start.
  4. Wait about a minute and a half A progress bar runs while six pieces are written. Do not unplug it.
  5. Connect it to your wifi When it restarts it makes its own little network called bunbun-XXXX (the letters change from toy to toy). Join it from your phone or laptop — the password is below — and a page opens asking for your home wifi. That is the last step; after this the toy runs on its own and the cable can go away.

One last thing — tell it the time, once

Right after it joins your wifi, tap the clock on the toy and set the current time. You only ever do this once.

It knows how to get the time from the internet, but it will not use it until somebody has shown it what the time is where you live — otherwise a toy in Texas would confidently display London time. That one setting teaches it your timezone, and every restart from then on syncs itself without asking.

Symptom if you skip it: the toy is on wifi, and the clock is still wrong.

This erases everything on the toy

Any pet living on it now is gone for good, along with its age, its name and any wishes still waiting on it. There is no undo. If this toy already has a pet somebody cares about, stop here and ask Jon first.

When it is finished

You get a penguin in a five-room world — a farmhouse, a kitchen, a washroom, a building site and a beach — with a cat that comes to visit and a clock on the wall. The egg hatches when someone taps it.

That world is a starting point, not a limit. Once the toy is on your wifi you can build your own rooms in the browser and send them over, and the pet keeps its age and name when you do.

Works in Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS and Linux. If the button says your browser is unsupported, that is the reason. Firmware 0.1.291.
